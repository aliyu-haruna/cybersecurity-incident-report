Academic Prototype — simulated authentication and browser localStorage. Not for production use.

User Management

View prototype accounts, review their roles, and activate or deactivate access.

Accounts are stored in this browser only. Passwords are plain text in the prototype and cannot be edited here; there is no password hashing, no account creation, and no server-side identity service. Deactivating an account blocks sign-in and removes that investigator from the list of people who can be assigned work.
